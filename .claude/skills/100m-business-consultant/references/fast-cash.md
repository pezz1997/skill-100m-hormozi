## Core Concept

**Fast Cash Plays** are limited-time, high-ticket offers marketed to existing customers, past customers, and engaged leads that compress the highest profit into the shortest time period.

### Core Characteristics

- **Timing:** Quarterly (every 90 days)
- **Scarcity:** Limited spots (5-10 available)
- **Exclusivity:** High-touch, limited availability
- **Price:** 10-50x normal price
- **Duration:** 7 days or less (or until sold out)
- **Value Type:** Unscalable (1-on-1 attention, personalization, access, speed, convenience, status)

### Why Fast Cash Works

Traditional business models have customers paying 1x the normal price repeatedly. Fast Cash Plays take 10% of your customer base and charge them 10x the normal price, generating 2x total revenue in a single week—with 90%+ margins—because you already spent the acquisition cost.

## Key Frameworks

### Framework 1: Fast Cash Plays Framework - Core Definition & Purpose

**Definition:** Limited-time offers to warmest audiences (current customers, past customers, engaged leads)

**Purpose:** Make the most money in shortest period possible from most likely to buy

**Characteristics that define a Fast Cash Play:**

1. **Limited Spots (Scarcity)**
   - 5-10% of customer base
   - If 100 customers: 5-10 spots
   - If 1,000+ customers: May be constrained by fulfillment
   - Goal: Sell out FAST (makes next offer more compelling)

2. **High Touch (Exclusivity)**
   - Personal attention from you or senior team
   - Premium service level
   - Accessible to limited number only
   - Not scalable (which is the point)

3. **Big Ticket (10-50x Normal Price)**
   - Normal $1,000 transaction → $10,000-$50,000
   - Normal $300/month → $3,000-$3,600 annual prepay
   - Price selected for profit, not volume

4. **Limited Time (7 Days or Less, or Until Sold Out)**
   - Creates urgency
   - Deadline forces decision
   - Sold out quickly (stampede effect in automated model)

5. **Unscalable Value (The Differentiator)**
   - 1-on-1 attention
   - Personalization
   - Access
   - Speed
   - Convenience
   - Status
   - Duration
   - These cannot scale (and don't need to)

### Framework 4: Fast Cash Offer Structure - Components

**Basic Structure:**
- 1-3 core components of main offer
- 3 escalating bonuses (revealed across promotion period)

**Why Layer Bonuses:**
- Refreshes attention across 7-day promotion
- Increases likelihood to buy
- Expands perceived price-to-value discrepancy
- Creates multiple "reasons" to buy

**Bonus Availability:**
- All bonuses available to all buyers
- Early buyers don't get exclusive bonuses
- All buyers get full package
- Early buyers just get confirmation of smart choice

**Example Offer Structure:**
```
Core Offer:
- 90-day 1-on-1 coaching program
- Custom strategy session with founder
- Weekly accountability calls

Bonus 1 (Day 6 of promotion):
- $5,000 premium templates package

Bonus 2 (Day 2 of promotion):
- VIP access to private community
- Priority response time (<2 hours)

Bonus 3 (Day 1, 24-hour mark):
- Personal cell phone access for emergencies
- Direct email to founder for questions
```

**Bonus Philosophy:**
- Each bonus should address pain point or desire
- Stack bonuses to create urgency (reveal across days)
- Best bonus worth more than total price (revealed last)

### Framework 5: Scarcity & Spots Framework

**How Many Spots to Offer:**

**Initial Cap: 5-10% of customer base**
- If 100 customers: 5-10 spots available
- If 500 customers: 25-50 spots available
- If 1,000+ customers: May be constrained by fulfillment

**Fulfillment Constraint:**
- Can you personally deliver to 10 customers in one week? If yes, 10 spots.
- Can you deliver to 100 customers? Probably not (even with team help)
- Constrain by what you can actually deliver excellently
- If you oversell, quality suffers, reputation suffers

**Goal: Sell Out FAST**
- Sold out in 3-4 days = good
- Sold out in 1-2 days = excellent (makes next offer more compelling)
- Not selling out = offer not compelling or price too high

**Waitlist Alternative:**
- If you consistently sell out, maintain waitlist
- Notify waitlist when new spots open
- Charge immediately when spot opens
- Permission-based: "Can I notify you if spots open?" → charge immediately if they say yes

### Framework 6: Two Selling Models - Different Promotion Sequences for Each

These are two fundamentally different models with different promotion timelines.

#### Model A: One-on-One Consultation Model

**Best for:**
- Service businesses (consulting, coaching, design)
- When you (or team) need to have consultation conversations
- When deliverable is strategy/planning session

**How It Works:**
- Make available day 1 for immediate purchase
- Drive people to book appointments (spread out consults over 7 days)
- Limited by sales staff capacity (how many consults can you handle?)
- Urgency: Limited spots + first-come-first-serve

**Timeline:**
- 7-day window to spread out consultations
- Schedule consultation calls throughout week
- Collect payment upon booking (or before consultation)
- Track real-time how many spots remain

**Advantages:**
- Conversion higher (personal touch from sales call)
- Relationship starts immediately
- Can customize offer during consultation
- Easier to handle payment objections

**Disadvantages:**
- More labor-intensive (sales calls)
- Limited by team's availability
- Harder to scale (can't do 100 consultations in a week)

---

#### Model B: Automated Checkout Model

**Best for:**
- Larger brands with big lists
- Volume-based businesses
- Businesses where offer is clear (no consultation needed)
- When you want massive urgency/scarcity

**How It Works:**
- Build tension with launch sequence
- Open cart to everyone at once
- First-come-first-serve stampede
- Sell out in minutes/hours (if offer compelling)
- Automated: No sales calls needed

**Timeline:**
- 5-day promotion (vs 7-day)
- Cart opens at specific time
- Sales tracking in real-time
- Update customers as spots remaining

**Advantages:**
- Highly scalable (10+ spots vs 5-10)
- No sales team needed (fully automated)
- Creates massive FOMO (stampede effect)
- Faster cash in (instant checkout vs booked consultations)

**Disadvantages:**
- Higher variance (some offers flop, some explode)
- Less relationship building (purely transactional)
- Lower conversion rate (need perfect offer/hooks)
- Requires good email/text list and tech

### Framework 7: Promotion Sequence Frameworks (2 Models with Specific Timing)

#### PUSH TO CONSULT SEQUENCE (7-Day Model)

This is the consultation model timeline and messaging:

**Day 7 (Sunday)** - Text Only - Teaser
```
"Tomorrow we're opening something very limited. Might be for you.
Check your email tomorrow morning."
```
- Purpose: Set expectation
- Channel: Text only
- Length: 1 sentence

**Day 6 (Monday)** - Announcement
```
Email: Headline your big offer, core components, price range, CTA to book
Text: "Offer open now - link to book consult in email"
```
- Purpose: Full reveal of offer
- Email: Include 1-3 core components, benefits, price, booking link
- Text: Drive to email (text draws attention, email has details)
- CTA: Book consultation appointment

**Day 5 (Tuesday)** - Recap + First Bonus
```
Email: Recap offer, reveal first bonus, social proof
Text: "Added a bonus yesterday. Last message before spots fill. Link in email"
```
- Purpose: Refresh attention, add urgency
- Add: First bonus reveal
- Social proof: How many booked, testimonials

**Day 4 (Wednesday)** - Optional (Use if Needed)
```
Only send if conversions are slow or you want additional boost.
Similar to Day 5 message (recap, new angle, social proof).
```
- Purpose: Mid-week push
- Only if campaign needs boost
- Can skip if spots filling

**Day 3 (Thursday)** - Sales Update
```
Email: Sales update (how many sold, how many spots left), remind first-come-first-serve
Text: "Update: X spots left. Book yours - link in email"
```
- Purpose: Create FOMO (fear of missing out)
- Update: Specific numbers ("7 spots sold, 3 left" vs generic "filling fast")
- Urgency: First-come-first-serve (whoever books first gets spot)

**Day 2 (Friday)** - X Spots Left + Second Bonus
```
Email: X spots remaining announcement, reveal second bonus
Text: "2 spots left. New bonus added to your email"
```
- Purpose: Higher urgency
- Update: How many spots remain (real number)
- Add: Second bonus reveal
- Scarcity: Getting tighter

**Day 1 (Saturday, 24 Hours Out)** - Warning + Best Bonus
```
Email: "Few hours left" or "X spots left" warning, add best bonus (worth > price)
Text: Multiple texts throughout day as deadline approaches
```
- Morning: "24 hours left - here's why this is worth it"
- Add: Best bonus (worth more than entire price)
- Anchor: "This bonus alone worth $X"
- CTA: "Book your spot now"

**Day 1 (Saturday, 12 Hours Out)** - Sold Out Announcement
```
Email: "Spots sold out"
Text: "Sold out - waitlist open if spots become available"
```
- Purpose: Finalize
- Message: Confirm sold out status
- Offer: Join waitlist (so you can notify of next opportunity)

**Day 1 (Optional)** - Back Out Spot Notification
```
If spot opens due to cancellation, immediately notify waitlist:
"A spot just opened. Reply YES to claim it or we charge your card on file."
```
- Charge immediately upon confirmation
- Clear permission (reply YES = charge)

## Formulas

### Formula 1: Revenue from Fast Cash Plays

```
# of Spots × Price Per Spot = Revenue from Fast Cash
```

**Example:**
- 10 spots available
- $6,000 price per spot
- Revenue = 10 × $6,000 = $60,000 in one week

**Scaling Example:**
```
Gym with 166 members at $150/month:
- Q1: 10 people × $6,000 = $60K
- Q2: 13 people × $5,000 = $65K
- Q3: 8 people × $6,000 = $48K
- Q4: 14 people × $4,000 = $56K
Total Fast Cash: $229K extra per year
```

---

### Formula 2: Margin Assumptions

**Fast Cash Plays: 90%+ gross margins**
- Example: $6,000 offer, $500 delivery cost = $5,500 gross profit (92% margin)
- Why so high? Acquisition cost already spent, variable costs low

**Recurring Business Baseline: 25% margins**
- Example: $300/month subscription, $225 cost = $75 profit (25% margin)
- Lower because includes all operational overhead

**Extra Revenue Drops Straight to Bottom Line**
- $60,000 Fast Cash revenue with 90% margin = $54,000 profit
- This is almost all bottom line (since fixed costs already covered by regular business)

**Impact on Business Profitability:**
```
Regular business: $300K annual revenue, 25% margin = $75K profit
Add Fast Cash Plays: $60K revenue, 90% margin = $54K extra profit
New total profit: $129K (72% increase)
```

---

### Formula 3: Pricing Formula

```
Normal Price × 10 to 50 = Fast Cash Price
```

**Examples:**

**Service-Based:**
- $1,000 transaction → $10,000-$50,000 Fast Cash offer
- $300/month subscription → $3,600 annual prepay (12 months)
- $500/month subscription → $30,000 for 5-year commitment (10x annual)

**Product-Based:**
- $100 product → $1,000-$5,000 premium version with service
- $50 course → $500-$2,500 with 1-on-1 coaching

**Determining Your Multiplier (10x vs 50x):**
- 10x = Conservative, safe, higher conversion likely
- 30x = Sweet spot for most businesses
- 50x = Aggressive, only for brand names or premium positioning

**Price Setting Strategy:**
- Start with 10x conservative estimate
- Test with small segment
- Based on response, adjust to 20-30x
- Goal: Select for people with purchasing power, exclude price shoppers

## Step-by-Step Processes

### Process 1: Fast Cash Offer Creation & Launch (7-Day Consultation Model)

This is the complete process from offer design to delivery.

#### Step 1: Identify Offer (Unscalable Value)

**What to do:**
- Think of craziest thing you can offer to your best customers
- Focus on unscalable value (refer to Framework 3)
- Don't think "product," think "experience"
- Ask: "What would customers pay 10x price for?"

**How to brainstorm:**
- Personal involvement (1-on-1 time is most valuable)
- White-glove service elements (cell phone access, concierge)
- Unscalable items (retreats, in-person experiences)
- Exclusive access (behind-the-scenes, mastermind)
- Premium treatment (VIP status, priority)

**Example offers:**
- "90 days of daily 1-on-1 coaching + done-for-you strategy" (consulting)
- "Done-for-you website build + ongoing support + personal cell access" (web design)
- "VIP fitness retreat + daily personal training + meal planning + networking" (gym)
- "Private mastermind + monthly 1-on-1 sessions + business strategy" (coaching)

**Reference:** $100M Offers playbook for deeper offer creation

#### Step 2: Determine Spots to Sell

**Calculate spots:**
- Cap at 5-10% of customer base
- If 100 customers: 5-10 spots available
- If 500 customers: 25-50 spots available
- If 1,000+ customers: Limited by your fulfillment capacity

**Fulfillment constraint reality:**
- How many customers can you personally deliver to in one week?
- Multiply by team members' capacity
- This is your realistic upper bound
- Better to sell out and overpromise is disaster than undersell

**Decision logic:**
- Start conservative (5 spots)
- See how easily you sell them
- Next quarter, increase or maintain based on fulfillment ease
- Track: Are you hitting deadline? Is delivery quality good?

#### Step 3: Set Price

**Formula:**
- Normal transaction/annual price × 10-50 = Fast Cash Price

**Decision matrix:**
- **10x price:** If you're uncertain, conservative offer, smaller list
- **30x price:** Sweet spot for most businesses, best conversion likelihood
- **50x price:** Aggressive, premium brand, proven offer

**Example prices:**
```
Normal: $1,000 transaction → Fast Cash: $10K (10x) or $15K (15x) or $30K (30x)
Normal: $300/month → Fast Cash: $3,600 (annual prepay, 10x) or $9,000 (30x premium tier)
Normal: $100 course → Fast Cash: $1,000 (10x) or $3,000 (30x 1-on-1 version)
```

**Pricing psychology:**
- Price selects for right customers (those with budget)
- Excludes price shoppers (good riddance)
- Higher price = higher perceived value
- Builds confidence in buyer ("must be amazing if it's $15K")

#### Step 4: Prep Bonuses (1-3 Core Components + 3 Escalating Bonuses)

**Core offer structure:**
- 1-3 main components (the core value)
- 3 bonuses revealed across 7 days
- All bonuses available to all buyers (not exclusive to early buyers)

**Bonus philosophy:**
- Each bonus addresses pain or desire
- Escalate in value (best last)
- Last bonus worth more than price
- Bonuses solve objections and increase perceived value

**Example structure:**
```
Core Offer (the main deliverable):
- 90 days of weekly 1-on-1 coaching
- Personal strategy session with founder
- Custom implementation plan

Bonus 1 (Day 6, when offer launches):
- $5,000 value templates and frameworks package
- (Reveal with main offer to anchor value)

Bonus 2 (Day 5, next day):
- VIP access to private community
- Priority response time (under 2 hours)

Bonus 3 (Day 1, 24-hour warning):
- Personal cell phone access for emergencies
- Direct founder access for questions
- Annual retreat invitation
- (This bonus worth $10,000+ alone, worth more than entire price)
```

**Execution:**
- Write down each bonus
- Assign reveal day
- Prepare copy for bonus reveals
- Ensure you can deliver all bonuses

#### Step 5: Create Promotion Schedule (7 Days Out)

**Setup (Week Before):**
- Schedule calendar: Set 7-day timeline
- Prepare email copy (see next step)
- Prepare text messages (shorter versions of emails)
- Create booking link (if consultation model)
- Test email/text delivery

**Day 7 (Sunday):**
- Send text: "Something big coming tomorrow"
- 1 sentence, text only
- Sets expectation for day 6 email

**Day 6 (Monday):**
- Send announcement email + text
- Email: Full offer, bonuses, price, booking link
- Text: "Offer open now - see email"
- Track: Bookings/clicks

**Day 5 (Tuesday):**
- Send recap email + first bonus reveal + text
- Email: Recap offer, reveal first bonus, social proof
- Text: "Added bonus, spots filling, see email"
- Track: Additional bookings

**Day 3 (Thursday):**
- Send sales update email + text
- Email: How many sold, how many left, first-come-first-serve reminder
- Text: "X spots left, book yours - link in email"
- Track: Urgency spike

**Day 2 (Friday):**
- Send X spots left email + second bonus + text
- Email: Specific number remaining, reveal second bonus
- Text: "2 spots left, new bonus, get email"
- Track: Increased urgency

**Day 1 (Saturday, 24 hrs):**
- Send warning email + best bonus + text
- Email: "Few hours left," best bonus (worth more than price)
- Multiple texts throughout day
- Track: Final push bookings

**Day 1 (Saturday, 12 hrs):**
- Send sold out announcement
- Email: "Sold out"
- Text: "Spots gone"
- Track: Final confirmation

#### Step 6: Write Copy for Sequence

**Copy formula for each email:**
```
[Attention Grab/Headline]
[Value Promise - What they get]
[Specifics - 1-3 main components]
[Social Proof - Who else bought, results, testimonials]
[Bonuses - What's included/new bonus reveal]
[Urgency/Scarcity - How many spots left, deadline]
[Call to Action - Click to book/buy]
[Signature - Personal touch]
```

**Example opening email (announcement):**
```
Subject: We're Opening 7 Spots (But Not For Long)

Hi [Name],

I'm running something this week that I only do quarterly,
and I wanted to give you first shot at it.

For the next 7 days, I'm taking on just 7 clients for an intensive
90-day coaching program that's normally not available.

Here's what included:
- 90 days of weekly 1-on-1 coaching with me personally
- Custom strategy session ($5K value)
- Accountability calls every week

Most people pay $30K for this. This week only: $6,000.

If you're serious, book a spot here: [LINK]

Spots are first-come-first-serve, so move fast.

Talk soon,
[Your Name]
```

**Copy best practices:**
- Lead with benefit (what they get)
- Follow with specifics (numbers, details)
- Add social proof (if available)
- Create urgency (spots, deadline)
- CTA (clear action)
- Personal tone (conversational, not corporate)

#### Step 7: Send Campaign

**Execution:**
- Blast to existing customers (core list)
- Blast to past customers (lapsed users)
- Blast to engaged leads (those who engaged but haven't bought)
- Exclude cold prospects (different marketing, lower conversion)

**Tracking:**
- Log all bookings in spreadsheet
- Note booking time and customer name
- Update customers on # of spots remaining
- Confirm bookings within 24 hours

**Timelines:**
- Spread consultations throughout 7-day window
- Schedule earliest consults first (earliest booker gets first available time)
- Aim to get everyone booked before deadline
- Leave some buffer for last-minute bookings

**Payment:**
- Collect immediately upon booking (don't wait for consultation)
- Option 1: Card on file (auto-charge)
- Option 2: Send invoice, payment due before consultation
- Confirm payment before adding to calendar

#### Step 8: Post-Campaign

**Delivery:**
- You WILL work more for these customers (that's the point)
- Premium delivery justified by premium price
- Personal involvement is expected
- Do not cut corners on delivery

**Collection of 4Rs (After Delivery):**
- **Results:** What did they achieve?
- **Reviews:** Get testimonial/review
- **Referrals:** Ask for referral introductions
- **Resells:** Can they buy again? Offer recurring package?

**Cool Down Period:**
- After Fast Cash Play, cool down your list (2-4 weeks)
- Go back to providing value
- No more selling for a while
- Build goodwill for next quarter's Fast Cash Play

**Schedule Next Round:**
- Plan next Fast Cash Play for exactly 90 days out
- Use learnings from this round
- Increase price, spots, or offer complexity if worked well
- Same time next quarter (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)

## Actionable Tactics

### 1. Offer Selection Tactics

**Interview Customers: "What would you pay 10x price for from us?"**
- Not: "What should we sell?"
- But: "What would be worth 10x our normal price to you?"
- Listen for: Personal involvement, speed, access, convenience

**Focus on Your Personal Involvement (1-on-1 Time is Most Valuable)**
- People pay for you specifically
- "Coaching with [Your Name]" > "Our team"
- Personal brand increases value
- Make this the centerpiece of offer

**White-Glove Service Elements**
- Cell phone access
- Concierge service
- Priority response
- Personal attention
- These justify 10-50x pricing

**Unscalable Items**
- Retreats
- In-person experiences
- Exclusive access
- Mastermind groups
- Network connections
- These create premium perception

---

### 2. Pricing Tactics

**10x to 50x Normal Price is Correct (Not "Too Expensive")**
- Psychological: Price = quality
- Financial: Justifies unscalable value
- Confidence: Signals premium offering
- Selection: Attracts right customers, repels tire-kickers

**If Normal Transaction is $1,000, Price at $10K-$50K**
- $1K → $10K-$50K range
- $500 → $5K-$25K range
- $100 → $1K-$5K range
- Use 10x if conservative, 30x if confident, 50x if proven

**If Normal Monthly is $300, Price Annual Prepay at $3,600**
- $300/month × 12 = $3,600
- Can also offer $6,000-$9,000 for premium tier
- Frame as "annual prepay discount" (even though it's just annual pricing)

**The Shock of Price is Point**
- High price selects for right customers
- Excludes bargain hunters (good, they churn)
- Creates confidence ("must be amazing")
- Builds perceived value

---

### 3. Bonus Reveal Tactics

**Hold Best Bonus for 24-Hour Mark (Worth More Than Entire Price)**
- Arrives when urgency highest
- Worth more than everything combined
- Should alone justify entire purchase
- Example: "Personal cell access for emergencies worth $5K alone"

**Reveal Bonuses One at a Time Across Days**
- Day 6: Show offer + first bonus
- Day 5: Second bonus
- Day 1, 24hr: Third bonus (best one)
- Refreshes attention throughout promotion
- Multiple "reasons" to buy across 7 days

**Make Bonuses Address Customer Pain**
- Speed (fast response, next-day delivery)
- Experience (white glove, special treatment)
- Access (personal cell, direct contact)
- Status (VIP badge, special recognition)
- Duration (longer access, ongoing support)

**All Bonuses Available to All Buyers**
- Don't create scarcity on bonuses
- Everyone who buys gets everything
- Early buyers just get confirmation of smart choice
- Don't penalize late buyers (bad customer service)

---

### 4. Scarcity & Urgency Tactics

**Cap Spots Low (5-10) So You Actually Sell Out**
- 5-10 spots, not 50
- Real scarcity = fast sales
- Fake scarcity = jaded customers
- Better to sell 10 spots at $6K than 50 at $1.2K

**First-Come-First-Serve Rule (Creates Stampede in Automated Model)**
- Whoever clicks first gets spot
- Creates competitive energy
- Fear of missing out (FOMO)
- Drives sales in rush

**Announce Every Milestone**
- "Half sold" ("5 of 10 sold, 5 remaining")
- "Almost gone" ("2 left")
- "Sold out" (final closure)
- Real numbers create credibility
- Transparency builds trust

**Set 7-Day Maximum Window**
- Consultation model: 7 days
- Automated model: 5 days max
- Creates deadline urgency
- Prevents drawn-out promotions
- Quarterly rhythm keeps it fresh

---

### 5. Promotion Tactics

**Use TEXT + EMAIL Combo**
- Text draws attention (highest open rate)
- Email has details (longer copy)
- Text: 3-5 words, clear CTA
- Email: Full story, benefits, bonuses

**Include Specific CTA in Every Message**
- Not: "Check email"
- But: "Book your spot here: [LINK]"
- Not: "Let me know if interested"
- But: "Reply YES to claim your spot"
- Specificity increases conversion

**Benefits-Focused Messaging**
- Not: "We're offering coaching"
- But: "Here's the transformation you'll experience"
- Good stuff gained: Freedom, results, success
- Bad stuff avoided: Stress, confusion, failure
- Lead with benefits

**For Consultation Model: Emphasize Scarcity of Your Time**
- Not product scarcity (generic)
- But: "I only take 7 clients per quarter"
- "My time is limited"
- "Direct access to me is the bottleneck"
- Personal scarcity > product scarcity

---

### 6. Exclusion Tactics

**Only Promote to Warm Audiences**
- Current customers (best)
- Past customers (second best)
- Engaged leads (third best)
- Definition of "engaged": In contact within past 6 months

**Exclude Cooled-Off Leads**
- Not contacted in 6+ months
- Relationship dormant
- Different marketing needed
- Focus on warm market

**Exclude Cold Prospects**
- No relationship
- No prior engagement
- Not right for Fast Cash
- Different funnel (offer first)

**Warm Audience = Highest Conversion, Lowest Cost to Reach**
- Example: 10-20% conversion on warm list
- Example: 0.5-2% conversion on cold list
- Fast Cash designed for warm market only

---

### 7. Timing & Cadence Tactics

**Run Every 90 Days (Quarterly) - Sweet Spot for Audience**
- Q1 (Jan-Mar)
- Q2 (Apr-Jun)
- Q3 (Jul-Sep)
- Q4 (Oct-Dec)
- Same timing each quarter (consistency)

**More Frequent = Audience Fatigue**
- Monthly offers = audience tunes out
- Perceived as desperate
- Devalues offer
- Burns through audience too fast

**Less Frequent = Missed Revenue Opportunity**
- Quarterly is minimum
- Proven sweet spot
- Audiences expect them
- Can sustain indefinitely

**Plan Calendar: Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 (Consistent Cadence)**
- Mark calendar 90 days in advance
- Plan offer during previous quarter
- Execute during designated quarter
- Build predictability and rhythm

---

### 8. Delivery Tactics

**You WILL Work More for These Customers (That's the Point)**
- Premium price = premium service
- Expected: Your personal involvement
- Expected: White-glove treatment
- Expected: High responsiveness

**Premium Delivery Justified by Premium Price**
- $6K investment = premium service level
- Not your regular offering
- Special treatment
- Worth the price

**Personal Involvement is Expected**
- Cell phone access promised
- Better honor it
- Response time promised
- Better honor it
- This isn't scalable (good, it's not supposed to be)

**Do Not Cut Corners on Delivery**
- If you promise 1-on-1, deliver 1-on-1 (not delegated)
- If you promise daily contact, deliver it
- If you promise personal cell, be available
- Reputation depends on delivery
- These customers become your best testimonials/referrals

---

### 9. Payment Collection Tactics

**Have Cards on File Ready (Charge Immediately)**
- Fastest payment collection
- Reduces payment friction
- Charges immediately upon booking
- Clear terms

**If No Card on File, Collect Immediately Upon Agreement**
- Don't wait for consultation
- Collect before or right at start of consultation
- Options: Invoice, Stripe link, PayPal, wire

**Options: Reply to Email with "I'm In," Text "Yes," Click Cart, Book Consult**
- Multiple payment methods
- Low-tech is fine (email reply)
- High-tech is fine (automated cart)
- Whatever works
- Make it easy
